      Inside the fly

      Memory against smell, in the real circuit

      A is where the fly remembers a store. B is a whiff: odor switches on hΔC cells that read the wind, and they write an upwind goal into the same goal cells, through their FlyWire synapses. Drag either around the compass; pull it toward the center to weaken it.
