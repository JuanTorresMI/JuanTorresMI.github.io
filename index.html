About

I study logistics at Central Michigan, work at Meijer, and build things on the side.

I’m a senior at Central Michigan University studying Business Administration with a concentration in Logistics Management. I carry a 3.70 GPA, a 4.00 in the major, and I graduate in April 2027.

Most of my work has been hands-on. I ran equipment on a factory line at FIAMM, and for the last couple of years I’ve been a certified meat cutter at Meijer, where a lot of the job is keeping inventory right, rotating stock and keeping the department clean enough that inspections come back with nothing on them.

When something from class sticks with me, I like to build it and see what happens. That’s how I ended up racing a fruit fly’s brain against Google’s routing software, and writing a price ledger for a Minecraft economy I trade in. Both run on this site. Outside of that, I’m in the Supply Chain Management Association and I start for CMU’s varsity League of Legends team.
