About

I study logistics at Central Michigan, work at Meijer, and build things on the side.

I’m a senior at Central Michigan University studying Business Administration with a concentration in Logistics Management, and I graduate in April 2027.

Most of my work has been hands-on. I ran equipment on a factory line at FIAMM, and for the last couple of years I’ve been a meat cutter at Meijer, where a lot of the job is keeping inventory right, rotating stock and helping train new people.

When something from class sticks with me, I like to build it and see what happens. That’s how I ended up racing a fruit fly’s brain against Google’s routing software, and writing a price ledger for a Minecraft economy I trade in. Both run on this site. Outside of that, I play jungle for CMU’s varsity League of Legends team.
