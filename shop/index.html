How buying works

eBay or CollX?
Either: most cards are on both. eBay takes cards, carts and offers the usual way, with eBay’s money-back guarantee; the store’s buy-more deal takes up to 15% off when you buy several. CollX is the card collectors’ app, where you can send an offer on any card.
Shipping
Cards under $20 ship in a plain envelope; $20 and up ship with tracking.
